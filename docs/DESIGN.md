# Design

**Register:** the look and feel of [The OM Project](https://ejhong.github.io/om/) — earth / wabi-sabi, small type, an instrument
more than a magazine — carrying a body drawn as sacred art: a figure made of light in an ink-stone viewing card.

- **Surfaces:** rice paper `#faf7f2` page; stone-grey top band `#e0dbd4` with the title and a line of scripture in Georgia italic;
  rounded cards (12px) with 1px `#e0dbd4` borders; ink-stone panels `#2a2826` (head `#232220`, body `#3d3a36`) for notes and
  contents, with small SF Mono text.
- **Type (system only, no web fonts):** `-apple-system…` sans for UI and headings (title 20px/700, section 15px/700);
  Georgia for prose (13.5px on paper, 12.5px in panels); SF Mono for labels, data and annotations (9–11px).
- **Colour roles:** terracotta `#c87868` = knots; sage `#7ca67c` = release; stone `#90989e` = trees and structure;
  plum `#a482ac` = the interstitium and the traditions; bronze `#9a8a78`/`#6b5d4d` = the hand (controls, play button).
- **Layout:** the instrument — a visualization card with a player bar (▶, timeline with markers, time, settings gear) beside a
  notes panel — for the introduction (a guided tour of twelve chapters) and the atlas (a life, breath, tools). Reading pages pair a
  paper card with an ink-stone contents panel.
- **3D:** "ink stone" ground by default (the figure as ~100k points of light), "rice paper" as the alternate (ink stipple).
  Mandorla halo, dust, bloom only on ink stone. Breath-paced motion.
- **Marks:** a small seal 結 in the title; a procedural ensō for breath and loading.
- **Honesty in the interface:** representative things say so; hypotheses are labelled as hypotheses.
