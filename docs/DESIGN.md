# Design

**Register:** sacred art — a dark temple, a figure made of light. Zen restraint: negative space, few elements, slow motion.

- **Themes:** night (default) — ink `#0e0f12`, moon ivory `#e9e4d8`; paper — washi `#f3f1ec`, sumi `#1c1c20`.
- **Accent:** shu (朱) vermilion for knots only. Gold for release (stars, pulses). Maps use traditional Japanese colours:
  ai 藍, moegi 萌黄, yamabuki 山吹, fuji 藤, toki 鴇, sabi 錆.
- **Type:** Cormorant Garamond (display, light), EB Garamond (reading), IBM Plex Mono (labels, numerals, annotations),
  Shippori Mincho (CJK glyphs). Small caps are mono, uppercase, tracked 0.14–0.18em.
- **Marks:** the seal 結 (musubi, to tie) in white on vermilion; a procedural ensō (`src/lib/enso.ts`) for breath and ornament.
- **Motion:** breath-paced (4 s in, 6 s out). Nothing snaps; things arrive over ~1 s.
- **3D:** the body is its perforators — a relief-lit stipple of ~100k points over an occluding floor; a mandorla halo behind the
  figure; dust in the void; bloom only at night and only on embers and stars.
- **Honesty in the interface:** representative things say so; hypotheses are labelled as hypotheses.
