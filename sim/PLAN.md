# The field guide: how knots would behave, theory by theory

*Status (28 Sep 2026): G0 done (this plan; the exam, the matrix and the author's words off the site). G1 is live and
G3's main part is done: the engine (`knots_sim/guide/`) runs all seven theories (perforators with conducted dilation
in their trees, the vascular latch, trigger points, densification, nerves, the motor switch and perception) through
ten scenes in 32 settings each, and the Hypotheses page shows each theory's account beside what its model shows, with
the instrument at the top (`/simulation/` redirects there); each theory's portrait is generated from the runs
(`findings/guide.md`); G2 is done: the Research page leads with where the seven part, generated from the guide. Next: the rest of G3, then G4. The first version of this phase,
an exam that scored the theories against the author's reports, is in [archive/](archive/2026-09-28-exam-plan.md); what
it built is kept (§9). The planning brief it started from is [BRIEF.md](BRIEF.md).*

## 1. The aim

People who work with the body find knots: small places that hold for years and let go in a breath, under a hand, or to
attention, sometimes with a spark. Nobody has recorded one letting go. The site gives seven answers to what a knot is.

This phase asks each answer to show itself. Each theory, run as a model from its own physiology and its own sourced numbers,
grows its own knots in the same patch of body, meets the same scenes (a stressful moment, slow breathing, attention, a hand,
rolling, an hour of moods, the years), and shows **how its knots would feel, respond and move**. Anyone who knows knots can
then look and recognise. Where the theories' knots differ, a measurement can decide, and the differences say which one.

It should be beautiful, because it is looked at; and useful, because it ends in a measurement.

## 2. Why this way round

The exam (26–28 Sep) wrote the author's reports as tests with sharp readings ("within 10 s") and scored every theory against
them. It was careful work, and it ran the wrong way round for what is known:

- **Predictions before comparison.** A theory that grows its knots from its own physiology, blind to anyone's reports, and
  then matches experience has said something. One scored against the reports is shaped by them, reading by reading.
- **Recognising beats specifying.** Reports of knots are rough impressions. They are good at "that one is like mine" and
  poor at "exactly when does it let go". The field guide asks the question people can answer.
- **It reaches beyond one person.** Anyone can compare their own knots with the same portraits, in their own terms.
- **The differences design the measurement.** Where two theories' knots differ in something an instrument can see, that is
  the experiment, and the guide says what each would record.

What carries over: the models, their sourced parameters, the *cannot* results, the instrument models, the discipline (§8).
What is retired: the exam as the organising idea (its versions, readings, pass shares, matrix and seals), and the author's
own words on the site: they were answers to questions, not written for presentation.

## 3. What the site will show

**Simulation: the field guide.** One ink-stone instrument:

- *The same patch for every theory*: 4 cm square over the upper trapezius (skin, fat, the superficial and deep fascia,
  muscle), with stress held more toward the neck side; one spot where a hand rests or attention goes; a sham place away
  from it. Each theory places its own units where its anatomy puts them (§4), so how many knots a patch can hold, and
  where, is itself a result.
- *Scenes* (§6), the same for every theory, played as films with the breath drawn beneath them, and a clock that runs from
  seconds to hours.
- *Side by side*: the four modelled theories' patches playing the same scene at once.
- *Up close*, one theory at a time: the patch from above; **beneath**, a cross-section of the knot at the spot, animated by
  its model's state (a vessel shutting, a band contracting, a motor unit latched and the neuron that holds it, or quiet
  tissue and a glowing body map); **under the hand**, what a hand would feel (the bump, tenderness, the spark); **on the
  instruments**, what a recording would show (skin flow at the spot and the sham, muscle activity, stiffness).
- *Its character*, generated from the runs, never written by hand (§7): what a knot is, how it feels, forms, holds,
  responds, lets go and moves, what it does with time, what an instrument would see, and what would rule the theory out.
  Each trait is marked: in every plausible setting; in some, and what that depends on; in none; silent; or not modelled.

**Research**, for researchers: what physics allows and rules out; where the theories part (the tells, generated); the
measurement ladder, cheapest first (§11); what is needed; the method, parameters and sources.

**Hypotheses**: each card links to its theory in the field guide. **Atlas** (G4): the whole body driven by the models,
theory by theory, in place of today's illustration.

## 4. The theories

| | Theory | Where its unit is | Its switch | Model |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 結 | Perforators | a small artery where it pierces the fascia; trees of a parent and its children, every 4–5 mm | the wall's own mechanics: open or shut over a band of tone (burton1951) | built |
| T2 閂 | Vascular latch | small arteries in skin and muscle, every 5 mm (smooth muscle anywhere) | a held prediction kept by the clamp that cuts its region off from awareness (Johnson's loop; the latch-bridge economises it, hai1988) | built |
| T3 点 | Trigger points | a contraction knot in a taut band, a few per muscle along the zone where its nerve enters | the energy crisis at an endplate (gerwin2004) | built |
| T4 膠 | Densification | the gliding layer between the fasciae, in 5 mm patches | a thixotropic layer with a critical stress: it rebuilds at rest and breaks under shear, so between two drives both a jammed and a fluid state hold (coussot2002) | built |
| T5 神経 | Nerves | a nerve of the skin where it pierces the fascia, beside its perforator (weum2016), a few in 4 cm | none: a sensitised segment (weeks: dilley2008) fires with its drive, the sympathetic route 14 s late (devor1994) | built |
| T6 覚 | Perception | a place on the body map, at its resolution | central gain, arousal and attention | built |
| T7 握 | Motor switch | a motor unit's territory in muscle, 5–10 mm, overlapping others | persistent inward currents that latch a unit on (gorassini1998, heckman2008), with its metabolic loop (johansson1991) | built |

**Are these the best contenders?** Reassessed 28 Sep 2026. Considered, and kept out as knots, with the reason:

- *Fascial contractility* (myofibroblasts, schleip2005): contracts and relaxes over minutes to hours; cannot let go in
  seconds. At most a slow variant of T4.
- *Scar, adhesion, fibrosis*: cannot let go in seconds. Kept as the control that should fail every fast scene.
- *Fat through a fascial opening, lipomas* (bicket2016): fixed lumps; nothing lets them go.
- *Local swelling*: no known route by which it comes and goes in seconds.
- *Arteriovenous anastomoses*: true switches that answer the breath, but in the skin of the hands, feet, ears and face,
  not over the back where knots are reported.
- *A spindle-driven contraction* (the muscle's own sensors driving its own motor units): the loop inside T7, a variant of
  it rather than a theory of its own.

**One addition**, as a variant of T7 (G3): *the motor switch with the vessel it squeezes.* Perforators often run through
muscle before they pierce the deep fascia. A latched unit raises the pressure in the muscle around one; when the unit falls
silent the vessel reopens and its patch of skin floods. It is the one combination of the fast switches that would give a
bump that unclenches *and* a spark over the skin. It rests on one unmeasured number: how hard a few latched units squeeze a
vessel beside them. With that, the seven accounts cover the serious contenders; no other theory is added.

**Each model, reassessed.**

- *T1.* Solid: the wall's balance from measured strength, thickness and pressure; the fold; the reflex timings; a vessel
  pressed shut cannot reopen until the hand lifts (a structural result). Weak: in healthy vessels it holds only at about 3.4
  times resting tone unless it sets over hours; it has no bump. In the guide: the patch becomes a small forest (parents and
  their children share pressure, so knots cluster and let go together as the tree says), and its tenderness (the
  territory's oxygen debt) and spark (its nerve's burst as blood returns) are recorded.
- *T3.* Solid: the energy crisis as its account states it; its press calibrated to the pressure-release literature
  (pecosmartin2019); the twitch. Weak: its slow sustaining factors (weeks) are not modelled; a nodule's stiffness is
  guessed. In the guide: a few nodules along the innervation zone, where its anatomy puts them.
- *T6.* Solid: gain, arousal, attention, pain inhibiting pain, the in-breath felt as less. Weak: one variant. In the
  guide: places at the body map's resolution, so its knots are broad and soft-edged; its tingling only from overbreathing.
- *T7.* Solid: self-sustained firing measured in people; set by serotonin and noradrenaline; switched off by inhibition.
  Weak: every number for knots is a guess. In the guide: territories along the fibres; a latched unit's fibres are
  scattered through its territory, so one unit is a diffuse firmness and a bump needs several together (the senses, §5);
  attention inhibits; a hand excites, or excites and then inhibits (variants).
- *T1's sleeve* (28 Sep, `models/sleeve.py`). The perforator model could not stiffen, yet knots are reported to limit
  movement. Each vessel passes the fascia in a sleeve of sliding tissue (guimberteau2005) that its flow keeps wet; shut,
  the sleeve dries, its hyaluronan crowds (viscosity as the third or fourth power of concentration, cowman2015), and it
  can jam (the gliding layer's thixotropy, coussot2002), pinning the layers at the vessel: a stretch meets a block, which
  cowman2015 ties to felt stiffness. The model's sharpest prediction: the spark comes as the vessel opens, but a jammed
  sleeve can stay stuck until the layer is sheared (a roller, a moving hand). Its drying and rebuilding times are guessed.
- *T2.* The latch-bridge economises; by itself it does not remember: relaxation follows calcium (rembold1991). In
  Johnson's account the memory is elsewhere, in the held prediction the clamp keeps out of awareness, so the guide builds
  that loop (`models/latch.py`, 28 Sep): stress writes a prediction; the prediction commands its region's vessels; the
  clamp keeps it written and cuts it off from awareness; attention or a hand lets it update, and the latch then relaxes
  over tens of seconds. The loop is set from the account's two claims (durable while unattended; ordinary stress alone
  does not latch a region) and where it sits between them is sampled; whether attention or a hand frees it is left to the
  model. Every number for knots is guessed; the latch's own rate constants (Hai and Murphy) would set how slowly a freed
  region warms. One formalisation among possible ones, for its proponents to check.
- *T4.* Built 28 Sep as the gliding layer's thixotropy (`models/densification.py`): Coussot's model of yield-stress
  materials with a cap on structure, so a still layer builds until it jams, movement breaks it, and between two drives
  both hold (the viscosity bifurcation, coussot2002). Checks against the account's own mechanism: hyaluronan alone does
  not gel (krause2001), hardly changes with pH in the body's range (gatej2005), and thins only about 2% a degree with
  warmth (cowman2015); so a structure that jams is more than hyaluronan (crowding with immobility, proteins bound in
  inflammation: cowman2015). Its knots are there before a scene (a still night, then a working day at the holding
  stress, frees the layer where it moves enough); they are broad regions, nothing firm, found by sliding the skin; a
  resting hand does not free them, a hand working the skin in small circles does in some settings, over tens of
  seconds, and ultrasound would see the layers slide again. The band's width is guessed; much wider bands would leave
  the whole layer stuck, which the account does not describe. The fascial chains along which it travels are not
  modelled.
- *T5.* Built 28 Sep (`models/nerve.py`): a few nerves of the skin where they pierce the fascia, each sensitised to its
  own degree (a neuritis takes weeks to come and go: dilley2005, dilley2008). Stress reaches a site through the muscle's
  load around it, at once, and through the sympathetic drive, about fourteen seconds late (devor1994, measured in
  injured nerves). No switch: a knot is felt while its drive lasts and returns, in the same place, when it comes back.
  Pressing excites it and, past a level, tingles along its branches: a tingle of pressing, not of release. Attention
  has no route. Its tell: a fixed point at a perforator's exit, silenced by a local anaesthetic (boelens2013).

## 5. The senses: from a model's state to what is felt

Written once (`knots_sim/guide/senses.py`, numbers in `params/senses.yaml`), the same rules for every theory; each theory
supplies what in its tissue is stiff, how stiff, how big and how deep.

- **The bump.** A hand feels stiffness through skin and fat. A stiff region of radius *a* at depth *d* is felt over a width
  of about √(a² + d²), and weaker the deeper it lies, as (a / √(a² + d²))³: the far field of a small stiff inclusion in an
  elastic half-space. Overlapping regions add. A bump is felt above a threshold (guessed). T1: a shut vessel is a fraction
  of a millimetre; nothing to feel. T3: the nodule. T7: each latched unit's territory, stiffened by the share of its fibres
  it drives. T6: nothing in the tissue.
- **Tenderness**, from each theory's own sensitiser: T1 the vessel's territory in oxygen debt; T3 its acid milieu; T6 the
  felt intensity above threshold; T7 its metabolites.
- **The spark.** T1 the nerve's burst as blood returns, over its patch of skin; T3 the local twitch; T6 overbreathing's
  tingling, everywhere at once, which a slow breath does not make; T7 none.
- **Letting go, felt**: how fast the bump and the tenderness fall at a release (at once, or over tens of seconds).
- **The instruments**: skin flow by laser speckle over the spot and the sham (odoherty2009, roustit2010); muscle activity
  (single motor units by surface EMG; a trigger point's endplate activity by needle); stiffness at depth (elastography);
  what is felt, for timing.

## 6. The scenes

The same for every theory, in the shared unit of stress (0 rest, 1 a surge, `hold` the stress that keeps knots), the slow
breath (4 s in, 6 s out), the hand (palpation pressure) and attention, as `knots_sim/exam.py` defined them; each theory maps
them onto its own inputs in its runner, written out for its proponents to check.

| Scene | What happens | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| A knot forms | rest; three minutes of stress; the stress eases to `hold` | how soon knots appear, where, how many; what is felt |
| Stress lingers | half an hour at `hold` | whether knots persist, grow, spread or fade |
| Slow breathing | thirty slow breaths | which let go, when in the breath, after how many, the easy first or not |
| Attention, no touch | thirty breaths with attention at the spot | whether the attended knot goes, and its neighbours |
| A resting hand | a hand on the spot for a minute with slow breaths, then lifted | when it lets go: under the hand, as it lifts, later, never |
| Rolling | a roller over a quiet place, once every 3 s for three minutes | whether pressing brings knots out, or frees them |
| After it lets go | the deepest knot at the spot worked until it goes; ten minutes more | whether others go with it; a new one nearby; one back in the same place |
| An hour of moods | stress wanders as moods do, with slow breaths | whether knots come and go |
| Rolling the knot | a roller over the knot at the spot, once every 3 s for three minutes | whether rolling frees it, as a foam roller is reported to |
| Warmth | five minutes of warmth over the patch, as in a hot shower, with slow breaths | whether warmth frees knots |
| Held for hours | stress held half an hour, or three hours, then gone | whether an old knot outlasts its stress |

Every scene runs in K plausible settings (Sobol over each theory's own parameter ranges, K = 32 to start). The film shows a
typical setting; the character (§7) speaks for all of them.

## 7. The character: traits generated from the runs

The same questions for every theory, answered per setting from its runs, then summarised:

- **In every setting** (all K): structural for that theory; its negation is what would rule it out.
- **In some**: with the parameter that best splits the settings where it happens from those where it does not, and which
  way; a number worth measuring.
- **In none**; **silent** (the account says nothing: no model runs on water); **not modelled**.

Traits set by the shared scale (the surge forms knots at the typical place; the holding stress keeps them) are
calibration: shown as such, never as findings. Numbers from guessed parameters are the map, not the finding; the words say
which.

**The reports and the models** (28 Sep, `guide/reports.py`): after the models have spoken, the Introduction's observations
(in the site's words) set against them: for each report and theory, in how many settings its knots do it, or why the model
cannot say. A recognition aid, not a score; nothing is fitted to it.

## 8. Rules

- **Fair.** The same patch, stress field, scenes, hand and breath for every theory; each theory in its strongest form
  (`src/data/hypotheses.ts`), with variants wherever the equations are a choice; each maps the scenes onto its own inputs
  in a runner whose mapping is written out.
- **Sourced.** Every parameter carries a `papers.json` id, a locator and the quoted line, or is marked guessed with a range.
  Papers enter the library only from PubMed's own records (`knots_sim.library`); quotes are checked
  (`knots_sim.params --verify`).
- **Generated.** Everything the site shows is written by `uv run python -m knots_sim.guide`; nothing is edited by hand, and
  every file names its run.
- **Honest.** Model output is labelled as model output; time compressed says so; a theory with nothing to feel says so.
- **Describe, never prescribe.** Scenes are things that happen to a model, not instructions. The moderation guidance stays
  on every page that shows release. No release cascades, and nothing that singles out the back of the head, on the
  prominent pages.
- **No author quotes on the site.** The accounts speak in the site's own words.

## 11. The measurement ladder (first draft; the tells will refine it)

Cheapest first. All descriptive: they observe what already happens, at the usual pace, under the site's moderation guidance.

1. *Noticing*, free: does a knot let go while a hand still rests on it, or as it lifts? Is it there before anything touches
   it, or does pressing bring it out? Can attention alone, without touch, let one go?
2. *Ultrasound* over a few knots, with colour Doppler: in which layer is the bump (fat, fascia, muscle); is a perforator
   beneath it; does the bump change when it lets go?
3. *Surface EMG that picks out single motor units*, with a button pressed at each felt release: does a unit fall silent at
   the moment it goes (T7), or does nothing in the muscle change (T1, T6)?
4. *A Doppler map against blinded marks*: do knots sit on perforators more than chance (T1, T5), on the innervation zone
   (T3), or anywhere (T6, T7)?
5. *Laser speckle at a release*, with a pressed sham (a press flushes every place it presses): does the skin over a knot
   flood as it goes (T1), or not (T3, T6, T7)?
